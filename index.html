EVERY TUESDAY & THURSDAY

BOGO NIGHT

Two nights a week, wings come in pairs. Here's how each deal works.

Tuesday

Traditional Bone-In Wings

The deal: Buy a 6-, 10-, or 15-count order of traditional bone-in wings and get a second order of equal value free. If you order takeout or delivery, or don't have a rewards account, the second order is 50% off instead.

How to get it: Dine-in only at participating locations, with a free Buffalo Wild Wings Rewards account.

Thursday

Boneless Wings

The deal: Buy one order of boneless wings and get a second order of equal or lesser value free.

How to get it: Available for takeout and delivery, and at some dine-in locations depending on the restaurant. No rewards account is needed.

Orders placed here get the deal automatically at checkout on BOGO days. Deal details can vary by location, so confirm with the restaurant.
